## Angle CHOSEN (copied pattern + modified)
Pattern copied from the field's best: give the agent a HUMAN ROLE it performs too well, then
land the punchline on the role's most overbearing behaviour. Modification: instead of the
saturated roles (middle manager, intern, CEO, parent-taking-credit), the role is a HELICOPTER
PARENT — overbearing and controlling, not dumb/slow/verbose, which is a fresh direction for
this field.
- Entry (89 chars, one line):
  "My agent is a helicopter parent — it watches me type and calls my mom when I skip lunch."
Why it should win: the frame is fresh (helicopter = 0 hits); the setup is short; the punchline
lands at the end ("calls my mom when I skip lunch" — specific, surprising, relatable); it
roasts the agent for caring too much / being controlling rather than for being dumb, which no
other entry does; playful and self-deprecating, never hateful; no banned stock joke
(hallucination / refusal / confident-wrong) anywhere in it.
